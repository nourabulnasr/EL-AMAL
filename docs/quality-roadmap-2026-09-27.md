## Acceptance targets and research
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): target AA contrast, keyboard access, unobscured focus, reflow and restrained/reduced motion. This is a target, not a conformance claim.
- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds): target LCP<=2.5s, INP<=200ms, CLS<=0.1 at the75th percentile. Lab scores and real-visitor measurements are separate.
- [Google site structure](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/help-google-understand-your-ecommerce-site-structure): crawlable categories/products, useful original content and accurate structured data. Demo records remain noindex and are not represented as real Product entities.
- [OWASP authentication](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authentication_Cheat_Sheet.html): strong credentials, access controls, generic failures and session safeguards.
- [OWASP uploads](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/File_Upload_Cheat_Sheet.html): private storage, allowlists, independent type validation, scanning and authorization before enabling uploads.
