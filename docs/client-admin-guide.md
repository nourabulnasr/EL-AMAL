# EL AMAL Admin Guide

Prepared for the EL AMAL owner and sales team by Nour Abulnasr.

This guide explains how to sign in, handle customer enquiries and uploaded quotations, maintain catalogue records, and identify the products visitors select most often. Use the private dashboard for daily work and the public website to check what customers see.

## Sign in and protect access

1. Open the administration address. Check for HTTPS and the exact website host www.al-amaleg.com.
2. Enter your assigned staff email and password, then choose Login. Obtain credentials privately from Nour; this guide deliberately contains no password.
3. After signing in, use Open product interest, Open demand reports, or Open stock control on the dashboard. You can also use the direct links above.
4. Use your own named account. Sign out when finished, especially on a shared computer.

Use a unique password of 15 to 128 characters. Keep it in a password manager. Five unsuccessful login attempts temporarily lock an account for ten minutes; wait before retrying rather than guessing repeatedly.

If access fails, first check the email, typing, keyboard language and Caps Lock. Do not send a password, reset link or verification token in WhatsApp or email to ask for help. Give Nour the page address, approximate time and error wording instead.

## Recover a forgotten password

Use Forgot password on the login page and enter the email attached to your staff account. This can differ from the Hotmail address that receives sales alerts. The site gives a neutral response to protect account privacy. Check the staff account mailbox, including Spam, and open the newest reset email. Follow the link and set a new unique password. You will normally return to the admin; sign in if prompted. Previous signed-in sessions are revoked.

Reset links expire after 30 minutes. Allow ten minutes before requesting another email for the same account. If the link has expired or was already used, request a new one. Nour can investigate delivery or account access, but the account owner should choose and enter the replacement password personally.

Activation and the final owner recovery check are recorded in the release checklist supplied with this guide. A generic response alone does not prove that an email was delivered.

## Find the most clicked products

1. Sign in and choose Open product interest, or open https://www.al-amaleg.com/staff/products.
2. Under Choose the view, select From and Through dates. Dates are inclusive and follow Cairo time.
3. Leave Language at Both languages, Device at session start at All devices, and Product context at All contexts for an overall view.
4. Set Sort by to Product selections, then choose Apply filters.
5. Read the first rows of Product interest. They are ordered by measured selections, highest first. Click a model name to open its public product page.
6. Compare Selected with Visible, CTR, Page views and Basket adds. A large click count is more meaningful when supported by enough visitors and actual enquiries.

Update every 30 seconds is enabled by default while this tab is visible. Use Refresh now for an immediate refresh or clear the checkbox to pause. The Last successful update time tells you when the displayed result was obtained. Updates pause in hidden tabs. If refresh fails, the previous results remain visible with a warning; do not treat that timestamp as current until refresh succeeds.

Choose Download CSV to save the same filtered aggregate view for Excel. The export contains product counts and rates, not customer contact details. Keep the export within the business team because it reflects business activity.

### Understand each column

| Column | What it measures | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visible | Consenting sessions where at least half a product card was visible for one continuous second | Compare the exposure each product received |
| Selected | Sessions that opened a product after a qualifying view in the same list and reporting window | Rank measured clicks from product lists |
| CTR | Selected divided by Visible, multiplied by 100 | Compare response to exposure, alongside the counts |
| Page views | Sessions with a qualifying view of the product-page heading, including direct visits | Compare total measured interest in detail pages |
| Basket adds | Sessions that activated an enabled Add to quote button | Identify consideration before an enquiry |
| Datasheets | Sessions that activated a technical-document link | See which models attract technical research |

For example, 12 Selected from 100 Visible gives a 12% CTR. This is an explanation, not a claim about current traffic. A dash means there is no measured denominator. Fewer than 30 Visible sessions receives a Small sample label; a 100% CTR from one session is not enough evidence for a purchasing decision.

The report counts distinct consenting 30-minute sessions per product, not every repeated click and not unique people. Fast clicks before the visibility requirement do not count toward CTR. Staff, recognized bots, visitors who decline analytics and browser privacy signals are excluded. Historical traffic before collection began cannot be reconstructed. Products with no measurements may have no row; absence does not mean the product is missing from the catalogue.

All contexts deduplicates the same session across lists. Do not add the individual context totals together. Use Product page views sorting when you also want to compare direct visits. Google search-result CTR is a separate metric in Search Console.

Choose Similar products under Product context to see activity from the suggestion cards on product pages. Use All contexts again when you want the overall product ranking.

## Compare clicks with actual enquiries

Open Demand reports at https://www.al-amaleg.com/staff/reports. Select dates and choose Verified customers under Enquiry group, then Apply filters. The report shows saved requests and requested quantities, grouped by model and range, with the largest requested quantity first.

Use this alongside Product interest. Many clicks with few enquiries may suggest that customers need clearer specifications or help selecting a connection. Frequent verified requests can support stocking discussions, but neither a verified email nor a basket addition is a sale.

Direct requests with a model typed by the customer are shown as Customer-specified model in the aggregate report. Open the individual enquiry to read its exact details. File-only quotations require staff to identify products from the document; the site does not guess quantities from uploaded files. Closed enquiries remain in historical demand. The same enquiry can appear in several rows, so do not add row request counts to estimate unique enquiries.

Both report screens support a maximum 93-day reporting window. Narrow the demand filters if its CSV export asks you to reduce the number of groups. Date and filter choices affect what you see; begin with broad filters before concluding that activity is missing.

## Handle a customer enquiry

Owner alerts are sent to alamal4trade@hotmail.com. The email identifies the enquiry; customer details and quotation files are reviewed after signing in to the private admin.

1. Open Enquiries and locate the reference from the alert, or review the latest records.
2. Read Request kind, Verification status, customer/company details, notes and submitted items.
3. For product enquiries, check the model, quantity, range and selected connection. Bottom and back connections can appear as separate lines.
4. Change Status to reviewing while checking the requirement. Record follow-up work in Internal notes and save.
5. Contact the customer through the business's normal sales process. Update Status as work progresses.

| Status | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| new | A request has been recorded and has not yet been reviewed |
| reviewing | A member of the team is checking the technical and commercial details |
| awaiting-customer | You need missing information or a response |
| quoted | The team has issued a quotation through its normal sales process |
| closed | The team has finished handling this request |

Changing a status does not automatically send a commercial quotation, reserve stock or place an order. Submitted customer details and item snapshots are read-only to preserve the original request. Use Internal notes for staff comments; those notes are not shown to the customer.

The customer must confirm their email. Unverified requests may be visible before they are ready for owner notification. Do not confuse test-verified records with verified customers. Check the current Notification queue for delivery status; the legacy Delivery status field in the enquiry is not the current email result.

## Review an uploaded quotation

The customer chooses Send an existing quotation on the public RFQ page, enters contact details, reviews them and confirms their email. They then attach supported files and choose Send files for review. Uploading alone does not finish submission.

1. In Enquiries, open the matching reference and confirm Request kind is quotation.
2. Check Quotation submitted at. A populated date indicates that the customer finalized the file submission; an empty date means the file request is not complete.
3. Open Enquiry attachments and match the enquiry/reference. Review the filename, type, size and expiry date.
4. Use the private download control. Follow any document-safety acknowledgement before downloading a PDF or workbook.
5. Review the file, clarify the required products with the customer and record progress in the enquiry's Status and Internal notes.

Files expire after 30 days. Save an authorized business copy to the company's agreed secure storage before expiry if the sales process requires it. Do not forward the private download link as a public sharing link. Catalogue-editor and warehouse accounts cannot read these private customer files.

Supported formats are PDF, Excel XLSX, JPEG and PNG. Legacy XLS, macros, password protection and external workbook links are unsupported. Photos are reconstructed by the server. Format validation is separate from antivirus scanning: follow the safety message shown by the download page and scan documents with the company's approved protection before opening them.

Customers can attach up to three files of 10 MiB each (10,485,760 bytes per file). A progress indicator shows the transfer. If the connection drops, keep the page open and retry Attach file within one hour to resume the saved parts. Discard selected file clears an unfinished selection; it does not delete a file already saved. Confirm the saved-file list before choosing Send files for review.

If upload capacity is unavailable, contact Nour with the reference and error message; do not delete unrelated customer records to make room. The website currently shares a 64 MiB logical attachment allowance across enquiries. This is an operating capacity limit, not an allowance of 64 MiB per customer.

## Check email delivery

Open Notification queue for alerts to the owner, and Verification emails for customer confirmation messages. Match the enquiry/reference and inspect the current status and attempts.

Pending means a message is waiting; processing means a worker is handling it. Sent means the provider accepted it, which is different from reaching the recipient's Inbox. Failed needs investigation. The scheduled delivery worker normally runs every five minutes. Allow for that interval and mailbox filtering before reporting a missing message.

Do not change queue timestamps, attempt counters or customer verification fields to force an email. Give Nour the enquiry reference and the displayed error. The receiving inbox is Hotmail; the sender address enquiries@al-amaleg.com does not itself create a mailbox you can sign in to.

## Maintain the product catalogue

Open Products, search for the printed model and open its record. Check the public English and Arabic pages after any approved update. Keep the model identity and external ID stable so existing links and analytics continue to refer to the same product.

Owners and catalogue editors can maintain catalogue records. Only the owner can publish reviewed products. A product requires English and Arabic names and descriptions, source evidence, a recorded reviewer/date and confirmed image/content rights before publication.

The Catalogue details field contains structured specifications, images, model availability and technical-document data. It is an advanced editor, not a free-text description box. Ask Nour to help with structural changes or new image assets; do not paste an unstructured paragraph into it. Verify every specification against the correct manufacturer model and datasheet.

The company-industry filters are separate from instrument categories. A model can serve more than one industry. The reviewed classification and its limitations are maintained in the site's catalogue logic; changing the CMS Applications selector alone may not change the public reviewed mapping. Ask Nour to review new industry claims rather than treating utility suitability as approval for direct food or pharmaceutical contact.

Public In stock or Out of stock is a dated catalogue availability statement. It is not a counted warehouse balance. Product suggestions are generated automatically from the same instrument category, vary by product and rotate daily; staff do not need to enter them manually. The three homepage models are selected in the website configuration, not through a homepage toggle in each product record.

## Use staff roles and stock control

| Role | Main responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| owner | Manage accounts, publish reviewed catalogue content, handle enquiries and reports, define SKUs and use stock control |
| sales | Review enquiries and private quotation files, use product-interest and demand reports, and permitted sales stock actions |
| catalogue-editor | Maintain catalogue drafts and categories; no customer files or product-interest reports |
| warehouse | Perform permitted stock operations; no private enquiry files or product-interest reports |

The owner manages named accounts in Staff. Assign the minimum role needed for each job. Contact Nour before removing the last owner or making an unfamiliar account change. Staff account deletion is deliberately restricted.

Stock control at https://www.al-amaleg.com/staff/inventory is for exact SKUs and configurations. It records receipts, reasoned adjustments, holds, release/expiry and dispatch in a permanent ledger. Do not invent opening quantities from the public In stock label. The owner must agree the counted balances and stock policy before the team relies on warehouse commitments.

Use the workflow's permitted controls and record a clear reason. Corrections are new ledger entries; historic movement records are not edited or deleted. An enquiry and a basket do not automatically reserve inventory. When a control is absent or access is denied, check the assigned role instead of sharing an owner's password.

## Daily and weekly routine

Each working day, review new verified enquiries and finalized quotation files, assign follow-up, update statuses and check any delivery failures. Keep customer data and downloads within approved business storage.

Each week, open Product interest for a consistent date range, sort by Product selections and compare those products with Verified customers in Demand reports. Record the period and filters alongside a CSV export. Look for a pattern across enough sessions before changing purchasing or promotion decisions.

Review stock availability dates and expired holds through the appropriate stock workflow. Tell Nour about factual catalogue corrections, broken technical documents or missing translations. Agree who owns domain renewal, backups and recovery so these tasks do not depend on one person's memory.

## Resolve common problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Product report is empty | Broaden dates and reset language/device/context filters. Only eligible, consenting sessions are recorded. |
| Your own clicks do not appear | Signed-in staff are excluded. Repeated clicks are deduplicated; do not generate artificial traffic. |
| CTR is 100 percent with few visits | Read the Visible count and Small sample label. Wait for more evidence. |
| Data stopped updating | Check Last successful update, the automatic-update checkbox, connection and staff sign-in. Use Refresh now. |
| Customer says a file was sent but the request is incomplete | Check Quotation submitted at. The customer must complete email confirmation and Send files for review. |
| An attachment expired | Request a new file through the approved workflow; expired content is not restored by reopening the old link. |
| Alert is missing | Match the reference in Notification queue, allow the worker interval, then check Hotmail Spam. |
| A menu is unavailable | Confirm the account's role and contact the owner or Nour. |

When asking for support, send the page address, enquiry or model reference, approximate Cairo time, and the visible error. Exclude passwords, reset links, verification tokens and unnecessary customer documents.
