## Step 2: Search Console — already connected

**Who: Nour. Client action is optional unless he wants his own access. Cost: free.**

Open https://search.google.com/search-console?resource_id=sc-domain%3Aal-amaleg.com.

The important buttons are:

- **Sitemaps:** the existing https://www.al-amaleg.com/sitemap.xml is successful. No need to submit a duplicate.
- **Pages:** explains which URLs are indexed and excluded. Check the report's update date. Forms, private pages, filters, redirects and made-up URLs should not all be indexed.
- **Inspect any URL:** paste a complete public URL and press Enter. If it says indexed, no action is usually needed. If an important public page is not indexed, check its live response and eligibility before requesting indexing. Do not request hundreds of filtered URLs.
- **Performance:** look at the search phrases, clicks, impressions, CTR and landing pages. Improve pages around relevant customer searches, not unrelated high-volume words.

The new About text is prepared on the review branch, not published. Request a recrawl for the changed About pages after its visual approval and production publication.
