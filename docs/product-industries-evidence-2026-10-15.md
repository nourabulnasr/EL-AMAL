# Product industry applicability and evidence

Reviewed on 15 October 2026. The company filters help buyers find relevant instruments while preserving pressure, temperature and accessories as separate product categories. A product can appear in more than one industry. An industry match is a discovery aid, not approval of a particular supplied configuration.

## Coverage

All 165 catalogue groups were considered: the 151-group September publication and the 14 additions in October. The resulting overlapping sets contain 62 petroleum groups, 13 pharmaceutical groups, 24 food and beverage groups and 164 general-industry groups. The September publication alone contains 56 petroleum groups, 13 pharmaceutical groups, 24 food and beverage groups and 150 general-industry groups.

MG-1 stays visible in the complete catalogue and under its instrument type, but has no company-scope assignment. Its manufacturer specifies medical-gas distribution and patient oxygen applications; this does not establish pharmaceutical manufacturing suitability.

## How the filters were assigned

The review uses the full Applications section of the archived manufacturer datasheets, the model-specific portfolio entries and the sources in the table below. The earlier importer read a truncated page summary and matched substrings. The new code uses an explicit list of exact model families. It does not derive classifications from product descriptions, stainless-steel construction, the word “chemical”, or a hazardous-area option alone.

Petroleum includes documented oil and gas, petrochemical, refinery and related process applications. TC83 and TC84 are included by inference from their specified sulphur-recovery and gas-to-liquids uses. TC10-K, TC12-A, TR10-K and TR12-A are replacement inserts for the corresponding manufacturer-specified complete process assemblies; their use note requires compatibility with the original instrument.

The named food applications include the listed thermocouple/RTD families, the TR75 thermometer, baking-oven control with SC15, TGS55/TGS73, the model 70 contact thermometer and PSD-4. A named industry is not a blanket sanitary certification. Process connections, thermowells, media, cleaning and contact requirements still depend on the chosen instrument configuration.

PSD-4 explicitly lists both food and pharmaceutical applications in the [manufacturer product page](https://www.wika.com/en-en/psd_4.WIKA) and PE 81.86 datasheet. PSD-4-ECO is kept separate; the PSD-4 review is not inherited by similarly named products.

Twelve other matches in food and pharmaceuticals are **refrigeration and plant utilities**, not direct product contact: TF-LCD, TC10-D, TR10-D, TR60, TF35, TF-2000, TF37, TF44, TF41, TF45, T91 and R-1. Their individual datasheets establish refrigeration or ambient/cold-room use. [WIKA's refrigeration industry guidance](https://www.wika.com/en-us/refrigeration_technology.WIKA) connects refrigeration systems with food cold rooms and pharmaceutical cold storage and specifically discusses R-1 and TF44. Extending this company-scope filter to the other listed refrigeration instruments is an application-based inference; every match carries the same bilingual utility-only note. This does not claim that every temperature variant suits ultra-cold storage.

General industry is the broad bucket for documented industrial instruments, supporting electronics and replacement parts. It is not an assertion of chemical, pharmaceutical or hygienic suitability.

## Corrections to earlier classifications

These existing groups lose an unsupported petroleum tag: TC53; TR53; SB15; DPGT40; DPGS40TA; DPGS40. TC53/TR53 engine oil sumps and the boiler/water/heating applications of the differential instruments are not evidence of petroleum-industry suitability. SB15 lists water, oil and gas as measurement media in heating/steam applications; those media alone do not establish a petroleum-industry application.

The review also restores petroleum matches that the truncated summaries missed, including refinery tubeskin thermocouples, several mechanical switch families and DIH50/DIH52. Brochure application evidence supports the petroleum classification of DSS34M/DSS34T even though their newer short datasheet descriptions use broader process-industry wording.

## Implementation and maintenance

The product-industries module supplies getProductIndustries(product) and bilingual getIndustryUseNote(product, industry, locale). Only a WIKA manufacturer record or an official HTTPS WIKA datasheet identity activates the model list. Similar names, demo models and other manufacturers do not inherit it. Unknown future products retain only recognised, reviewed CMS industry values. No database schema or stock values are changed.

The public catalogue should use the returned industry array. Industry notes should remain alongside product recommendations so utility matches cannot be mistaken for product-contact selections. New model families require a new evidence row and explicit mapping review; adding a broad keyword rule would undermine the distinction this change establishes.

The six focused tests cover all 165 groups, overlap and counts, misleading oil/boiler substrings, distinct PSD-4-ECO and MG-1 uses, exact-family matching, trusted-source gating, future CMS values and bilingual utility notes. They completed successfully locally on 15 October 2026. Full integrated route and interface verification belongs to the parent change.
